Basic Information on Plant Care

Plants require a few essential elements to grow well: sunlight, water, soil, and nutrients. Understanding the specific needs of your plants is crucial for their health. Here are some basics:

  • Light: Different plants have varying light requirements. Ensure your plants receive the right amount of sunlight.
  • Water: Overwatering is a common mistake. Check the soil moisture before watering.
  • Soil: Use the appropriate soil type for your plants to ensure proper drainage and nutrient availability.
  • Nutrients: Fertilize your plants according to their needs to promote healthy growth.
